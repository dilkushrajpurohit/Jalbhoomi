# 🌱 JalBhoomi — Smart Groundwater & Irrigation Management System

> **JalBhoomi** is an intelligent groundwater monitoring and smart irrigation platform designed to help farmers make better decisions before and after borewell drilling.

JalBhoomi combines **IoT sensors, groundwater data, weather information, and AI-based recommendations** into a single dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Farmers often face challenges such as:

- ❌ Uncertainty about groundwater availability before drilling
- ❌ No real-time monitoring of borewell water levels
- ❌ Over-pumping and unnecessary water usage
- ❌ Lack of soil moisture information
- ❌ Difficulty in monitoring borewell health
- ❌ No intelligent irrigation recommendations
- ❌ Limited access to groundwater and weather-based insights

JalBhoomi aims to solve these problems through a unified digital platform.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

JalBhoomi provides two major systems:

### 1. 🗺️ Pre-Drilling Intelligence

Before drilling a borewell, users can analyze groundwater-related information for a selected location.

Features include:

- 📍 Location-based groundwater analysis
- 🗺️ Groundwater availability visualization
- 💧 Groundwater level information
- 📊 Historical groundwater data
- 🌧️ Weather information
- 🌱 Crop and field information
- 🤖 AI-assisted drilling recommendations

---

### 2. 💧 Post-Drilling Monitoring

After a borewell is installed, JalBhoomi continuously monitors important parameters.

#### Sensor Parameters

| Parameter | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 💧 Water Level | Monitor borewell water level |
| 🌊 Flow Rate | Monitor water discharge |
| 🧂 Salinity | Monitor water quality |
| 🌱 Soil Moisture | Monitor soil condition |
| ⚙️ Pump Status | Monitor pump operation |
| 🌧️ Rainfall | Weather-based irrigation decisions |
| 🌡️ Temperature | Environmental information |
